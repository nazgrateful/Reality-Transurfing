# Reality-Transurfing

**Transurfing Path** (`index.html`) is a single-file study app for the ideas in Vadim Zeland's *Reality Transurfing*.

- **Lessons**: 18 short lessons in six parts (space of variations, pendulums, importance and balance, flow, intention and slides, goals and the soul). Each has key ideas, an exercise, a two-question quiz and a reflection prompt.
- **Practice**: eight daily practices to tick off, with a 16-week activity heatmap and streak.
- **Journal**: free entries plus lesson reflections, filterable by lesson.
- **Glossary**: searchable terms linked to their lessons.
- **Book tracker**: page and chapter in your own copy.

Progress is stored in `localStorage` when opened directly in a browser, and privately in your Claude account when opened as a claude.ai artifact. Back up or restore from the **Data** tab.

The lessons are plain-language study notes written for this app, not the book's text.

Open `index.html` in any browser; there is no build step.
