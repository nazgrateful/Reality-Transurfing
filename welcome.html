The pendulum

What you fight, you feed.

In Zeland's picture, a pendulum is any system that runs on people's attention: a feud, a feed, a fear. It swings on the energy you give it, for or against.

Drag the importance down, or let go, and watch the swing die out. Nothing was defeated. It just stopped getting fed.

The mirror

The world reflects what you hold, with a delay.

The reflection always arrives late, so people give up on a thought just before it comes back. Type something you believe about your world and see it returned.

Good things find me easily.

This is what comes back. Keep holding it.

Your slide
